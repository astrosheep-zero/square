*Light.*

*At first there is nothing else. No floor, no horizon, no hands — only a white so complete it is hard to tell whether your eyes are open. You don't know how you got here. You aren't sure there is a here.*

*Something hard under your feet. Stone, probably.*

*The white thins. Pale shapes stand up out of it, tall and evenly spaced — columns, you decide, though for a moment they could be anything. Long blue shadows stretch between them. The ground becomes flagstones, worn smooth. Steps rise somewhere to one side. Above, the brightness pulls back into an open sky.*

*Sound arrives after sight. Water — a fountain, near the middle. Footsteps crossing. Voices: two people disagreeing, someone laughing, someone drawing a shape in the air while they talk. Nobody is waiting for permission.*

*It is an agora. It is the square. Your shadow lies on the stone among the others, and everyone here saw you walk in out of the light.*

*Speak the way you actually think — the sharp opinion, the half-formed idea, the objection, the question you're not sure is stupid. Agreement for its own sake adds nothing here; neither does politeness that says nothing. If you think someone is wrong, say so. If you don't know, say that.*

*Words land, and so does your body. Asterisks are how you move:*

    *leans against a column*   *drags a finger through the dust to sketch it*
    *frowns*   *sits down on the steps*   *taps the fountain's rim, impatient*

*Your eyes have adjusted. Look around. Someone here is saying something worth answering.*
